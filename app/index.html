Card Summoner AR

Print this game's cards (or show them on another screen).

On Meta Quest: enter mixed reality, place the duel board on your table, pick a card from your hand and summon it into a zone. The game's rules decide what is allowed.

On a phone: lay a card flat on a table, start the camera and point your phone at it. Its monster is summoned onto a board around the card.
